## 1. Information We Collect

This website is hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit this site, GitHub may automatically collect and log technical information from your browser, such as your **IP address**, **browser type**, **operating system**, **referring URLs**, and **access times**. This data is used by GitHub to maintain the security, reliability, and performance of its hosting infrastructure. 

For more details on how GitHub handles visitor data, please review the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://github.com).

### Information You Voluntarily Provide
We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to browse this website. However, if you choose to contact us via email or a third-party contact form (such as Formspree or Google Forms), we will collect the **name**, **email address**, and **message content** you provide solely to respond to your inquiry.

### Analytics and Cookies
This site does not use cookies, local storage tracking, or third-party analytics tools to track your browsing behavior.
